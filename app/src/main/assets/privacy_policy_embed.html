🔒 Privacy Policy — Royal Shield

Last updated: February 23, 2026

1. Introduction

Royal Shield is committed to protecting your privacy and the data you entrust to us. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information when you use Royal Shield:

  • Account & Contact Information: name, email address, phone number (only when voluntarily provided).
  • Device Information: device identifiers (e.g., Device ID), operating system, app version, language, and crash diagnostics.
  • Security Activity Data: URLs scanned, applications analyzed, threat/breach alerts, and security-related events.
  • Location Data: only with your explicit permission, for features such as S.O.S., security tracking, and Family/Child Tracking.
  • Family/Child Tracking Data (if enabled): real-time or background location updates, geofence settings, and notification events (e.g., “entered zone”, “left zone”).

3. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used to:

  • Provide and improve Royal Shield’s security services.
  • Detect threats, malware, or harmful content.
  • Send security alerts and updates.
  • Process payments for subscriptions and donations (where applicable).
  • Respond to user inquiries and support requests.
  • Family/Child Tracking (if enabled): provide real-time location visibility to authorized parent/guardian accounts, trigger geofence alerts, and support safety workflows.

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

4. Family / Child Tracking (Important)

Royal Shield offers optional Family/Child Tracking features designed for safety. These features are disabled by default and require explicit user action and permissions.

  • Consent & Authorization: a parent/guardian must authorize linking and tracking. Verification steps may be required depending on product configuration.
  • Permissions: location access (foreground and/or background) is requested only when needed. You can disable or revoke these permissions at any time in app settings.
  • What we collect: location coordinates, timestamps, and optionally accuracy/speed/heading to support safety alerts and service reliability.
  • Sharing: location is shared only with authorized parent/guardian account(s) you approve. We do not share this data for advertising.
  • Controls: parents/guardians can pause tracking, remove a linked child device, delete history (if stored), and manage geofences.

5. Data Storage & Protection

  • We use encryption and industry-standard safeguards to protect data in transit and at rest.
  • We use reputable cloud providers and security controls designed to limit unauthorized access.
  • Access to your data is restricted to authorized personnel only, and only as needed to operate and support the service.

6. Data Retention

We retain data only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, unless a longer retention period is required by law. Retention may vary by feature:

  • Live location (Family/Child Tracking): stored as needed to provide real-time service and reliability.
  • Location history (if enabled): retained for a limited period or until deleted by the parent/guardian.
  • Security events: retained to provide alerts, improve detection, and support troubleshooting.

7. Sharing of Information

  • We may share your data with service providers who assist in operating our app (e.g., payment processors, cloud hosting), strictly to provide the service.
  • We may disclose information when required by law or to protect safety and security.

We will never share your data for advertising purposes without your consent.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the data we hold about you.
  • Request correction or deletion (subject to legal requirements).
  • Withdraw consent at any time.
  • Disable data sharing features in the app settings.

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@royalshield.com.

9. Children’s Privacy

Royal Shield is not intended for use by children under 13 without a parent/guardian. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children without parental consent. If you believe a child has provided data without consent, contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted in the app or website with an updated effective date.
